❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What age is Baby Sensory for?
Baby Sensory is designed for babies and toddlers from 0–3 years old, under parental supervision. The bold visuals and sounds are especially engaging for very young infants.
Does the app work offline?
Yes! Baby Sensory works completely offline. No internet connection is needed to play.
Is Baby Sensory free?
Baby Sensory is free to download with all core scenes included. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases.
How do I switch between scenes?
Tap the menu button (⊞) in the top-right corner of the screen to open the scene selection menu. Choose any scene to switch instantly.
My app is crashing. What should I do?
Try closing and re-opening the app. If the issue persists, please restart your device. For ongoing issues, contact us with your device model and OS version.
Does the app collect any data?
No. Baby Sensory collects zero personal data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
