Frequently asked questions

What’s free and what’s paid?

The first 5 puzzles are free. A single one-time purchase unlocks all 100 puzzles for good, with no subscription and no ads.

How do I unlock every puzzle?

Tap any puzzle after the first five and answer the grown-up question. Then tap Unlock and confirm with your Apple ID.

I already paid. How do I get my puzzles back on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID you bought with. Tap a locked puzzle, answer the grown-up question, then tap Restore purchase. You won’t be charged again.

How do I turn the music off?

Tap the music note button in the top-left corner of the map. The app remembers your choice.

How do I turn hints on or off?

While playing a puzzle, tap the lightbulb button. With hints on, the game gently shows where a piece goes if your child pauses.

Where are the grown-up settings?

On the map, press and hold the top-right corner. From there you can let your child play the puzzles in any order, start over, or unlock every puzzle.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

Does the app collect any data?

No. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. See our Privacy Policy.
