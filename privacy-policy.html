🔒 Our Commitment to Privacy

Baby Sensory was built with babies and parents in mind. We believe in keeping things simple, safe, and transparent. This app is designed to be a completely private experience with no data collection whatsoever.

🛡️ Baby Sensory collects absolutely no personal data.

📋 What We Collect

  • No names, emails, or contact information
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No usage analytics or crash reports sent to us
  • No location data
  • No camera or microphone data

📱 On-Device Only

All interactions within Baby Sensory happen entirely on your device. There are no servers, no databases, and no cloud storage involved. The app works 100% offline and does not communicate with any external services.

👶 Children's Privacy

Baby Sensory is designed for use by infants and toddlers under parental supervision. We fully comply with children's privacy regulations (COPPA, GDPR-K). Since we collect no data at all, there is nothing to protect — by design.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this policy, the new version will be published here. We will never introduce data collection without being completely upfront about it.
