Privacy Policy

Little Wooden Puzzles

Effective date: October 18, 2026

The short version

Little Wooden Puzzles does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app never connects to our own servers.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect or send any personal information, such as names, email addresses, photos, location, contacts or device identifiers.

Information kept on your device

To work properly, the app saves a few settings only on your device:

  • which puzzles have been completed
  • whether music and hints are turned on
  • whether the full game has been unlocked

This information never leaves the device, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app removes it.

In-app purchase

The app offers one optional, one-time purchase to unlock all puzzles. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple’s handling of your information is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Purchases are protected by a parental gate, so a child cannot start one alone.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no social media features. It has no links that take a child out of the app.

Children’s privacy

Little Wooden Puzzles is made for young children. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children. This is in line with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR and the App Store Kids Category rules.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If the app ever started collecting data, we would ask for any consent the law requires first.
